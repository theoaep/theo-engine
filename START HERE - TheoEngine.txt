TheoEngine
==========

TO INSTALL ON WINDOWS:
1. Double-click "INSTALL THEOENGINE.bat".
2. This is a file-copy-only installer; it does not change the registry, download software, or require admin.
3. Restart After Effects and open Window > Extensions > TheoEngine.
4. If the unsigned CEP panel is not shown, follow the manual Adobe CEP setup in docs\INSTALL.txt.

TO INSTALL ON MAC:
1. Make the scripts executable in Terminal:
   chmod +x "INSTALL THEOENGINE.command" "UNINSTALL THEOENGINE.command" installer/*.sh
2. Double-click "INSTALL THEOENGINE.command".
3. Restart After Effects and open Window > Extensions > TheoEngine.

UNINSTALL:
- Windows: double-click "UNINSTALL THEOENGINE.bat".
- Mac: double-click "UNINSTALL THEOENGINE.command".

The installers only copy or remove the TheoEngine CEP folder. They do not touch browser cookies, localStorage, presets, or projects.
