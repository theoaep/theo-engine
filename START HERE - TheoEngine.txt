TheoEngine
============

TO INSTALL:
1. Double-click "INSTALL THEOENGINE.bat".
2. Choose Y when asked to install the After Effects panel.
3. Fully restart After Effects.
4. Open Window > Extensions > TheoEngine.

Brave is optional. The installer asks separately before downloading it.

Need help? Open docs\INSTALL.txt.

You do not need to open or change anything inside the extension or installer folders.
